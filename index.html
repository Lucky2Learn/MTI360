Automate · Governed AI

AI that works with your team — not around it.

MTI 360 uses governed AI to assist staff, students and management. Agents never touch your database directly — they act only through authorized tools, respect every permission, and leave an audit trail.

  1. 01
    AI Agent
    Receives a request from staff, a student or a workflow
  2. 02
    Authorized Tool
    Only tools the role is permitted to use
  3. 03
    Business Service
    Same rules and validations your team follows
  4. 04
    Tenant Data
    Scoped to your institute — never shared across tenants
  5. 05
    Audit
    Every action logged: who asked, what ran, what changed
