# MTI 360

## Maritime Training Institute Growth & Operations Platform

**Positioning:** The Complete Growth & Operations Platform for Maritime Training Institutes

**Tagline:**
**Acquire Students. Simplify Operations. Grow Your Institute.**

**Product Definition:**

> **MTI 360 is an AI-powered Growth & Operations OS that helps Maritime Training Institutes acquire students, convert admissions, run training operations, manage compliance, improve placement and automate everyday business activities.**

---

MTI 360 is a multi-tenant SaaS platform designed specifically for Maritime Training Institutes.

# 7. PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

## 7.1 Student First

Every process should ultimately improve the student experience.

---

## 7.2 Growth First

MTI 360 should not be positioned only as an ERP.

It should actively help institutes:

* generate leads
* qualify enquiries
* improve counselling
* automate follow-ups
* increase admission conversion
* understand campaign performance

---

## 7.3 Automation First

Repetitive activities should be automated wherever practical.

Automation may use:

* rules
* workflows
* scheduled actions
* communication
* AI agents

---

## 7.4 AI Assisted, Human Controlled

AI should assist staff and students.

AI must not receive unrestricted access to the platform.

High-impact operations should have appropriate:

* permissions
* confirmation
* approval
* audit
* human handover

---

## 7.5 Compliance Aware

The platform should support maritime training compliance workflows and traceable records.

Regulatory rules should remain configurable where appropriate.

MTI 360 should be **DGS-aware but not DGS-dependent**.

---

## 7.6 Integration First

MTI 360 should integrate with external systems where appropriate rather than attempting to replace every external platform.

---

## 7.7 Multi-Tenant by Design

Tenant isolation is a fundamental product and security requirement.

Each institute must have strict separation of:

* data
* users
* documents
* communications
* AI knowledge
* AI executions
* analytics
* workflows
* configuration

---

## 7.8 Reusable Platform

Common platform capabilities should be reusable across future verticals.

Maritime-specific capabilities should be implemented as configurable or vertical-specific capabilities wherever appropriate.

# 15. PLATFORM VS TENANT ADMINISTRATION

Platform administrators and tenant administrators are separate security domains.

## Platform Administration

Controls the SaaS platform.

## Tenant Administration

Controls the institute.

A tenant administrator must never receive platform-level access simply because they are an administrator within their institute.

Platform access must be explicit.

# 16. CONTROLLED SUPPORT ACCESS

Platform support users may access tenant environments only through explicit controlled support mechanisms.

# 98. PRODUCT DECISION PRINCIPLE

When deciding whether a capability belongs in MTI 360, consider:

1. Does it create meaningful value for Maritime Training Institutes?
2. Does it improve acquisition, admissions, operations, compliance, placement or growth?
3. Can it be integrated without compromising the core platform?
4. Can it be implemented securely in a multi-tenant environment?
5. Can it be operated sustainably?
6. Can it be reused across appropriate customers?
7. Does it create unnecessary complexity for the current stage?

The product should prioritize customer value over feature volume.
